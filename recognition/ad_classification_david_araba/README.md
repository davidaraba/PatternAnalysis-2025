# Alzheimer's Disease Classification with []

## Description

This project aims to solve the problem of classifying Alzheimer's disease using brain scan images from the ADNI dataset. The goal is to distinguish between two classes: Alzheimer's Disease (AD) and Cognitive Normal (CN).

The solution is implemented using a GFNet architecture, a modern deep learning model for computer vision tasks. This implementation trains the model on the provided dataset and evaluates its performance, aiming for a minimum accuracy of 80% on the test set.

## What is Alzheimer's Disease?

Alzheimer's disease is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder, meaning it causes brain cells to degenerate and die over time. It is the most common cause of dementia—a general term for a decline in cognitive ability severe enough to interfere with daily life.

The disease initially affects the parts of the brain associated with memory, leading to its most well-known early symptom: difficulty remembering new information. As it progresses, Alzheimer's leads to more severe symptoms, including disorientation, mood and behavior changes, confusion about events, time, and place, and difficulty with speaking, swallowing, and walking.

At a biological level, it is characterised by the buildup of abnormal protein deposits in the brain: **amyloid plaques** and **tau tangles**. These plaques and tangles disrupt communication between nerve cells and trigger their death, leading to the cognitive and functional decline seen in patients.
